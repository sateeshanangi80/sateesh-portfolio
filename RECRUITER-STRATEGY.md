## Before each application

1. Read the job description. Identify its core frontend requirements and compare them with work you can explain and demonstrate.
2. Tailor the title, short summary, skills order, and first two experience bullets to that job. Use the employer's terms where they truthfully match your experience. Keep official employment titles and dates unchanged.
3. Put the strongest relevant contribution first. Explain what you built, the engineering challenge, and the result. Only add performance improvements, adoption, time savings, or percentages when supported by evidence.
4. Use the employer's requested format. When none is specified, the PDF is the default. Check the application's parsed fields for name, email, work history, and education; correct errors before submitting.
5. Keep LinkedIn, GitHub, the portfolio, and resume consistent. Add the deployed portfolio to LinkedIn's Featured section and pin ResolveAI with an accurate README, current setup steps, and a short demo.
6. For a relevant referral or recruiter contact, write a short personal note naming the role and one matching example. Link the portfolio and attach the targeted resume if appropriate. Send one courteous follow-up if the channel allows it; avoid bulk generic outreach.
7. Track role, company, application date, resume version, referral, response, and next action. Review patterns in responses to improve targeting and evidence.

## Research behind the choices

- [CareerOneStop resume formatting](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx): single-column layout, conventional headings, readable fonts, PDF when no other format is requested.
- [Greenhouse: unsuccessful resume parse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse): images, columns, complex tables, headers/footers, and unclear sections can cause parsing problems. An extraction check is useful, but it does not certify every ATS.
- [UC San Diego resume guide](https://career.ucsd.edu/resources/guides/resume.html): specific contributions, action/task/result bullets, relevant keywords, strongest evidence first, and tailoring to the role.
- [Harvard: writing a resume and cover letter](https://extension.harvard.edu/blog/how-to-write-a-great-resume-and-cover-letter/): accomplishment statements, defensible quantification, customized applications, and networking.
- [W3C: Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html): respect motion preferences and allow nonessential movement to be disabled.

## Verification boundaries

Build/type validation, PDF extraction/render review, and local browser checks can establish local quality. They cannot predict recruiter decisions, certify all ATS products, prove project business metrics, or confirm public deployment and social previews.
